  summary: "OODA-Tool argues that ReAct-style policies suffer state-action competition: interleaving state tracking and action generation in one autoregressive trajectory lets the pressure to emit the next call overwrite accumulated context. Borrowing Boyd's OODA loop, it routes each decision through controller-checked typed states — Observe reconstructs state, Orient decides whether to act, Decide forms an admissible action, Act emits it.
