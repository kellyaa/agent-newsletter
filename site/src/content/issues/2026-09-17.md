  author: "Yipeng Liu, Yingqiang Zhang, Feifei Li, Huanchen Zhang"
  score: 8
  tags:

  - "tool-use"
  summary: "The insight is sharp: agentic requests idle for wall-clock time during tool calls while their KV cache pins GPU memory, and serving systems decide eviction by guessing call duration from name, history, or declared timeouts. The paper shows no pre-call estimate can rank durations — but the running tool already holds the answer. A harness that surfaces per-call progress recovers a readable signal across four public corpora at no benchmark cost, cutting p90 TTFT after a tool call by ~20.7% against LRU, near oracle."
  takeaway: "If you run a serving engine behind agents, exposing tool progress as an eviction hint is a cheaper win than tuning a duration predictor."
