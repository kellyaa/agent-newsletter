  summary: "First systematic study of cost-inefficient behavior in coding agents, over 1,200 trajectories from Claude Code and Mini-SWE-Agent on SWE-bench Verified. Three recurring patterns — subsumed retrieval, similar-script generation, test re-execution — hit 79–98% of tasks and account for up to 22.75% of cost. Across 10k held-out trajectories, developer-designed skills cut cost by up to 41.73%, roughly double agent-synthesized skills, while structure-aware retrieval sometimes raised cost by 28%.
