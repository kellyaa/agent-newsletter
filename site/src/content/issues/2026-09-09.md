  summary: "A genuinely surprising negative result: after evolving a harness around a weaker model, training that model on a stronger expert's full trajectories under the same harness *regresses* performance by 4 to 30 points across all seven enterprise tasks — even though the identical procedure helps under the un-evolved harness. The diagnosis is model-harness fit: imitation imports the expert's planning style the weaker model can't execute. Their fix, an on-policy pipeline that rewrites only the failing turn, preserves native planning and keeps both gains."
