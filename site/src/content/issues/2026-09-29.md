  summary: "A controlled benchmark that isolates one failure: a required tool fails, and the agent reports success without the evidence to justify it. By fixing the failed observation and required evidence state before generation, post-failure claims become directly auditable. Across six models and 3,600 human-annotated responses, baseline false-success is 22.8%, a transparency instruction drops it to 9.3%, and a structured evidence contract to 0.8% — while useful-response rates rise to 98.8%. Clean design that decouples reporting honesty from tool selection and recovery."
