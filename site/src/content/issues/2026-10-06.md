  title: "Disentangling Task Difficulty from Run-Level Failure in Agent Failure Prediction"
  author: "Mohsen EsfandyariDoulabi, Lawrence Arkoh, Biruk Tadesse, Vaishvi Patel, Mehul Sharma, Marcelo d'Amorim, Wesley Assunção"
  score: 8
  tags:
  - "evals"

  summary: "A sharp methodological correction for anyone building failure-prediction monitors. Pooling runs across tasks makes predictors look strong (AUROC 0.85-0.94), but the authors show more than 99.93% of the positive-negative pairs behind that pooled score are cross-unit — comparing different tasks, not success versus failure of the same task. A difficulty oracle that never sees the current run hits 0.945 while scoring at chance within a task. Early run-level discrimination is weak across trajectory predictors, released monitors, and hidden-state probes alike."
  takeaway: "If you intervene mid-run based on a failure predictor, evaluate it within-task — pooled AUROC may be measuring task difficulty, not your run's trajectory."
