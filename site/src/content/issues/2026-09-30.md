  summary: "Agents mutate state across local files, tools, and remote services, and today's harnesses lack a unified way to undo it safely. Planarian introduces statepoints — consistent, restorable point-in-time versions spanning local and remote state. Snapshot uses incremental process and filesystem checkpointing locally while recording compensating actions for remote changes; rollback reverts and replays those compensations; fork branches isolated executions. The compensating-action model for services that don't support checkpoints is the clever part, since most external APIs won't ever expose one."
