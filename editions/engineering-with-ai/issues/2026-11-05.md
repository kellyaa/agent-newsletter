  summary: "Bloomberg reports field data from applying LLM-assisted migration pipelines across thousands of repositories: a Python packaging campaign (~1,900 repos) and a C++ build-system one (~6,700). Together they produced 2,333 PRs and 729 successful migrations. The architecture keeps processing deterministic and confines the LLM to bounded transforms within pre-computed context. The headline finding: generation was easy; the bottleneck was owning teams reviewing, testing and deploying. Verifying changes in-pipeline before opening a PR, and uniform populations where fixes carried over, cut that cost."
  takeaway: "Budget your migration effort around review and verification capacity, not generation, and push validation into the pipeline before a PR ever reaches a team."
