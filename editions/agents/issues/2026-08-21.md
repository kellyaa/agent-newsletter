  summary: "Fiducia-bench asks whether financial agents finish tasks within policy — escalating when obligated, abstaining when required, leaving an audit trail — and studies how decomposition affects that. The finding is sharp and mechanistic: policy-relevant facts discovered by one component get attenuated at the handoff boundary before reaching the component that must act. Across 626 episodes, a 32B model dropped 0% of facts single-loop, 56% in a fixed pipeline, 85% under orchestrator-subagent. A stronger model dropped 3-6%, so the cost is partly a capability function. Same mechanism yields both under- and over-escalation. Benchmark is open."
