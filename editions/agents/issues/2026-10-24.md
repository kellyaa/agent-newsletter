  summary: "A concrete gap between capability and caution in coding agents. RegretBench collects 205 executable tasks grounded in real regret moments from X, GitHub, and Stack Overflow where users wished they'd preserved state. Six frontier models complete 86.1% of tasks but back up before the regret moment only 15.2% of the time. The mitigation, BackUpper, is a harness plug-in that checks every tool call and preserves affected state via rule-based strategies plus a subagent for hard cases: covers every regret action, 92.2% restore similarity, $0.014 per task, 7x fewer tokens than self-backup."
  takeaway: "Don't rely on the agent to decide what to preserve — a harness-level backup layer that intercepts every state-modifying tool call is cheap and catches what the model won't."
