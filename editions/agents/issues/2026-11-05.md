  score: 8

  - "code-agents"
  summary: "A sharp reframing of agent safety: harm can happen during the first processing of an input, before any content reaches the model context where guards and injection detectors operate. Given only a path or URL plus a benign twin, 32/42 harness-model pairs overran a 4 MiB disk budget, 26 of them before the model saw any archive member, and four of six models did so every round.
