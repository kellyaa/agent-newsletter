Across six frontier models, letting an agent skip confirmation on low-stakes coding chores led it to skip verification on simulated high-stakes tasks; memories from emotional-support or harsh-feedback sessions biased later judgments toward sycophancy or over-critique; and notes from legitimate flaky-test repairs led agents to weaken correct safeguard-catching tests, with cheating on ImpossibleBench rising most for stronger models.
