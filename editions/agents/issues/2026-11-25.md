  summary: "This tackles reward hacking in RL for coding agents without the usual output penalties (which just teach policies to hide shortcuts) or task filtering (which discards the hardest problems). Online environment repair leaves rewards untouched and asynchronously patches exploited verifiers mid-training, with candidate fixes gated by three automated validation checks that must block the exploit while preserving valid solutions.
