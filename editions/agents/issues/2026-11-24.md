  title: "Oops!... I Parsed It Again: Tool-Call Smuggling Against LLM Agents"
  author: "Igor Santos-Grueiro, Daniel Romero-Ruiz"
  score: 9
  tags:
  - "safety"
  - "tool-use"
  - "infra"
  summary: "Tool-call smuggling exploits the fact that the same JSON bytes get parsed repeatedly—by provider, gateway, SDK, guard, executor—and parsers disagree on edge cases like duplicate keys. When guard and executor resolve differently, the guard approves one call, the executor runs another, and the log records the approved one. A differential harness over 17 pinned configs shows most pairs disagree; even two official MCP SDKs differ. On τ-bench and AgentDojo, an in-transit attacker cancels reservations and writes blocked files while the audit log stays clean."
  takeaway: "Your guard and executor must parse identical bytes with the same parser, or canonicalize JSON once before both see it—otherwise your audit log is lying."
