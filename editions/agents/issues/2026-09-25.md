  summary: "Most agentic memory curates at write time: a finished trajectory gets distilled into a fixed reflection or skill, then retrieved by similarity — forcing the system to decide what matters before the future query exists. This defers curation to read time, keeping raw trajectories and synthesizing a compact, task-adaptive payload once the current task is known.
