  summary: "This preregistered study compares free-text, structured JSON, and HMAC-SHA256-signed requirement manifests for multi-agent handoffs, with exactly scorable synthetic tasks. On Qwen3-8B across 1,440 trajectories, signed manifests cut unsafe commits under attack from 69.4% to 9.0% with no loss of conformant commits, and a deterministic verifier caught 78.7% of violations versus 59.0% for an LLM checker at far lower false alarms.
